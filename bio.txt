Ha tocado con artistas como Saulo Fernandes, Margareth Menezes, Paula Lima, Jair Oliveira, Gabriel Grossi, Aca Seca Trío y Nella Rojas, y ha grabado junto a Paquito D'Rivera, Bobby Shew, Luis Salinas y Perico Sambeat, entre otros.

Hoy forma parte de varios proyectos en Barcelona, con los que actúa en festivales y clubes de todo el mundo.

[en]
Argentine bassist based in Spain since 1999. His playing blends jazz, soul, pop, Brazilian music and Latin rhythms, with a sound defined by rhythmic depth, precision and sensitivity.

He has played with artists such as Saulo Fernandes, Margareth Menezes, Paula Lima, Jair Oliveira, Gabriel Grossi, Aca Seca Trío and Nella Rojas, and has recorded with Paquito D'Rivera, Bobby Shew, Luis Salinas and Perico Sambeat, among others.

On the Spanish scene he has shared the stage with Rita Payés, Marco Mezquida, Juan Gómez “Chicuelo”, José Reinoso, Albert Bover, Ernesto Aurignac and Raynald Colom, to name just a few. Today he is part of several Barcelona-based projects that take him to festivals and clubs around the world.

In 2026 he launched Portrait of Jaco, his own project devoted to the music of Jaco Pastorius, alongside Lluc Casares, Antonio Mazzei, David Xirgu and Juan Berbín.

He is an official artist of D'Addario, the world's leading string maker.
